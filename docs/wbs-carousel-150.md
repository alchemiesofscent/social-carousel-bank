## SERIES ARCHITECTURE

### Existing series
- **DEAD WORDS, LIVING SCENTS** — Core vocabulary. One word → one world.
- **FROM THE WORKSHOP** — Experimental reconstruction, process documentation.
- **MYRRHA** — Mythological mini-series (3 parts, complete).

### New series
- **THE RECIPE** — Full ancient perfume recipes, step by step. Dioscorides, Aetius, Paul of Aegina, Edfu.
- **THE MARKETPLACE** — Adulteration, quality testing, supply chains, fraud, pricing. Dioscorides + Galen.
- **TOOLS OF THE TRADE** — Physical equipment: presses, mortars, strainers, vessels, heat sources.
- **MATERIA** — Individual ingredients in depth. Theophrastus + Dioscorides as primary.
- **THE NOSE KNOWS** — Smell theory, philosophy of scent, sensory epistemology.
- **ARTS OF VENUS** — Perfume + luxury + desire + cosmetics + the body. Ovid, Pliny, Athenaeus.
- **THE TRANSMUTATION** — Perfumery ↔ alchemy. Shared vocabulary, shared tools, shared logic.
- **PHARMAKON** — Perfume as medicine. Hippocratic corpus, Galen therapeutic, aromata as drug class.

## NOTES

- Some posts appear in both a WP and a series that overlaps with another WP (e.g., rhodinon appears in both WP2 as recipe and WP8 as social context). These should be **separate posts** — recipe post is step-by-step, social context post is narrative.
- WP13 (alchemy) was the most speculative block in the original plan; it has since been completed from the local alchemy source dossier.
- THE RECIPE series should have a consistent internal structure: hook = "what is this perfume," body slides = ingredients + process, closer = what it tells us.
- MATERIA series should lead with sensory description, not botanical taxonomy.
- Buffer of 4 posts allows for merging overlapping topics or cutting weak entries during review.
