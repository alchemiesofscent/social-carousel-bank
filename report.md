# Full-Bank Writing Review of `carousel-bank/src/data/carousels.js`

Review date: 2026-03-24

## Method

This review covers the current published bank in `carousel-bank/src/data/carousels.js`: 128 entries across 9 series.

It follows the same method as the draft review in `workbench/report.md`:

- writing-only review
- no source-fidelity assessment
- focus on style, flow, audience fit, and accessibility/readability
- grouped findings plus a combined synthesis

The intended audience throughout is an academically serious but Instagram-native general reader. The standard applied is the repo’s own stated tone: discovery-led, direct, curious, and scholarly without collapsing into lecture mode.

## Combined Synthesis

### Cross-series strengths

- Hooks are the bank’s biggest strength. The project reliably leads with discovery, contradiction, scale, price, or weird material detail.
- The best entries translate scholarship into bodily, social, or sensory terms. That is the account’s strongest house move.
- The bank has a real voice. It is recognizably scholarly, confident, and curious, and when it is at its best it feels unlike generic history content.
- The strongest published entries are often older or structurally simpler: they usually do one clean thing and end before the reader tires.

### Recurring writing issues

- The main recurring problem is middle-slide density. Names, units, quotations, examples, and theoretical glosses often arrive together on one screen.
- Hooks are often stronger than bodies. Several posts open with a sharp promise and then cash it out through accumulation rather than shaped development.
- Closers too often summarize cleanly instead of producing a fresher last turn.
- Some later entries drift into slightly internet-coded or contemporary-marketing phrasing that sits below the bank’s best register.
- Several clusters reuse the same structure: correction hook, dense evidence slide, broader thesis closer.

### Accessibility and readability findings

- Proper-name load is a consistent friction point, especially in recipe, marketplace, and lexical posts.
- Greek and Latin work best when they are short, strategically placed, and immediately cashed out into consequence.
- Process-heavy temple and recipe entries often preserve too much note texture for mobile reading.
- The most readable posts usually do one clear thing per slide and convert lists into image, consequence, or scene immediately.

### Strongest exemplars

- Strongest hooks: `nenib`, `blind-perfumers`, `castor-linseed`, `rose-garland-medicine`, `root-cutters`
- Strongest flow: `plektikon`, `kyphi-solar-lunar`, `pliny-royal-perfume`, `perfumers-shade`, `moschos`
- Best audience fit: `scratchandsniff`, `piper-gold`, `cleopatra-regime`, `perfumers-wrist`, `rose-garland-medicine`
- Best closers: `castor-linseed`, `perfume-and-soul`, `perfumers-shade`, `mendesian-paul`, `stakte-definition`

### Highest-priority weak spots

- `falernian-scale`: biggest accessibility problem in the published bank; too many comparisons and too much nested explanation.
- `aetius-people`: best example of accumulation replacing shape.
- `hekenu`, `tisheps`, `medjet`: strongest examples of research-density outrunning carousel readability.
- `kyphi-edfu`: strongest recipe example of body text reading like compressed notes.
- `baryosmon-bromodes` and `izo-verbs-adulteration`: strongest lexical-overload examples.
- `hicesius-drinking-perfumes`: most list-like execution in the symposium/medical crossover material.
- `karyophyllon` and `new-spices`: strongest examples where the concept is good but the middle turns into inventory.

### Guidance for future drafting

- Apply a hard body-necessity check. If covering one body slide does not break the argument, cut or merge it.
- Limit unfamiliar proper nouns and technical terms on any one slide unless comparison is the whole point.
- Any list slide should cash out immediately into consequence, image, argument, or social meaning.
- Treat closers as reframes, not summaries.
- Prefer one vivid example over three compressed ones.
- Avoid modernized punchlines or internet rhetoric unless the entry is otherwise very plain.
- In dense ritual/process posts, choose one operative image and let the rest serve it.

## Bottom Line

The writing quality across the published bank is high. The project already has a strong hook engine, a distinctive scholarly voice, and a real talent for turning obscure material into readable surprise. The next level is not better topics. It is tighter middles, lower processing cost, and more closers that end on image or implication rather than clean summary.
