# Puzzle triage of the carousel bank

Date: 2026-09-26

Every one of the 183 carousels, sorted by one test: **does slide 1 break something the reader already expects?** A strange fact on its own is not enough. Where there is no real puzzle, the post is held, cut or folded into another one. No puzzles were invented to save a post.

**Result:** 48 Passage posts (absorbing 45 others), 3 Bench posts ready to pair, 27 held as surprises with no expectation to break (with 16 merged into them), 10 posts held as Bench material, 34 cut.

## Rules the hooks follow

- A puzzle needs an expectation the reader already holds ("expensive means complicated", "you judge perfume by smelling it"). Slide 1 breaks it. If the reader has no expectation, the hook is only a random fact, however strange.
- When the expectation isn't universal, slide 1 states it first, then breaks it.
- The puzzle must be in, or follow directly from, the source.
- The post answers it by the last slide, or says plainly that we don't know.
- Hooks are plain sentences: no em-dashes, no "It wasn't X. It was Y.", no citations on the slide.
- The hooks below are drafts to rewrite in your own voice. The answers are one-line notes, not copy.
- Items marked **Check** make a claim that needs confirming against the source before posting.
