# Puzzle triage of the carousel bank

Date: 2026-09-26

Every one of the 183 carousels, sorted by one test: **does the source give us a real puzzle or surprise for slide 1?** Where it doesn't, the post is cut or folded into another one. No puzzles were invented to save a post.

**Result:** 75 Passage posts (absorbing 61 others), 3 Bench posts ready to pair, 10 posts held as Bench material, 34 cut.

## Rules the hooks follow

- Slide 1 is a puzzle or surprise that is stated in, or directly follows from, the source. If a reader wouldn't ask "why?", it isn't one.
- The post answers it by the last slide, or says plainly that we don't know.
- Hooks are plain sentences: no em-dashes, no "It wasn't X. It was Y.", no citations on the slide.
- The hooks below are drafts to rewrite in your own voice. The answers are one-line notes, not copy.
- Items marked **Check** make a claim that needs confirming against the source before posting.

## Passage posts

Types: paradox (two things that shouldn't both be true), backwards value (the weak or cheap thing wins), impossible instruction, wrong name, missing piece, contradiction between sources, surprise.
