## What to emphasize

- visible hardware
- fitted parts
- gentle versus harsh heat
- clay-sealed joins
- receiving jars and suspended vessels
- workshop monitoring instead of abstract process terminology
- the broader shared instrument world of ancient ergasteria

- strong claims that perfumers used the exact same named tools
- file-name citations in publishable `subText`
