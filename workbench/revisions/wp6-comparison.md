# WP6 Guideline Pilot Comparison

Date: 2026-03-24

This document compares four states for the latest completed work package:

- current published version in `carousel-bank/src/data/carousels.js`
- prior draft revision in `workbench/drafts/2026-03-24-wp6/draft-r3.js`
- overflow revision in `workbench/drafts/2026-03-24-wp6-overflow/draft.js`
- new guideline-based pilot revisions in `workbench/revisions/2026-03-24-wp6-guideline-{a,b,c}/`

Method:

- writing-only comparison
- no source-fidelity judgment
- default preference for the lightest change that improves readability, flow, audience fit, or series distinctiveness

## Batch Result

- 9 of 9 WP6 entries received a guideline-based revision draft

The pilot result supports the minimal-change approach. None of the WP6 posts required a full rewrite. Most gains came from tightening body slides, reducing overlap, and preserving the strongest published hooks and closers.

## What The Pilot Changed

The successful changes were small:

- soften one inflated hook
- compress one overloaded list
- cut one example chain
- move the real payoff earlier
- restore one dropped distinguishing feature

The pilot did not support:

- full rewrites
- broader reframing
- more contextualization
- adding new examples to prove the point harder

## What This Means For Remaining WPs

Use the same editorial order of operations:

1. preserve strong hooks
2. preserve strong closers
3. fix the middle
4. cut one weak slide before rewriting two strong ones
5. protect series distinctiveness when neighboring posts overlap

WP6 passes as a working test case for the forward editorial guidelines in `docs/editorial-guidelines.md`.
