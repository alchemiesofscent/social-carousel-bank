## What to emphasize

- The difference between what heats, what applies pressure, and what holds the material.
- Process control: stoppering, stirring, soaking, indirect heat.
- Evidence-safe uncertainty around form.
- Concrete workshop verbs: squeeze, soak, warm, strain.

## What to avoid

- A generic “ancient presses existed” explainer.
- Declaring `organon` to be a screw-press.
- Turning `kyrtis` into a cloth bag.
- Treating the doubled vessel like a decorative perfume bottle.
