Two minor fidelity notes (not blocking):

1. Post 6 (susinum), slide 2: "10 pounds of lily petals -- trimmed and cooled -- replaced three times across separate infusions." The source (aet_paul.md lines 742-758) confirms 10 pounds of lily petals, three infusions (eis treis embolas), and three successive batches of lilies with three-day intervals.
