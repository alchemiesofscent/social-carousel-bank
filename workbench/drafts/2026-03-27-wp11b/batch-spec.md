## What to emphasize

- Smell as part of medicine’s evidence and mechanism.
- The practical difference between plain oil, perfume, and compound aromatic smoke.
- Air, head, and body treated as connected spaces.
- Strong boundaries on what the sources do and do not prove.

## What to avoid

- An “ancient aromatherapy” umbrella claim.
- Modern anti-inflammatory or antiseptic language without qualification.
- A sweeping plague-history carousel.
- Turning `scent-diagnosis` into a loose list of weird smells.
