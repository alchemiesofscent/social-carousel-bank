## What to emphasize

- Perfume as pharmaceutical form.
- Shock of medical route: fumigation, nostrils, ureter, topical application.
- The difference between “smells good” and “is medically chosen.”
- Physician judgment about which aromatic medium to use and why.

## What to avoid

- Generic “ancients also used aromatherapy” framing.
- Moralizing about strange medical procedures.
- Turning `please-the-sick` into a modern bedside-manner manifesto.
- Repeating the already-published kyphi or rose-garland arguments.
