## Source Fidelity Check

**PASS.** All five carousels draw claims directly from `diosc_smell.md`. Specific verifications:

- euodia-dysodes: Frankincense burning with euodia (Part 1, Section 1, ref 1.68.5); agallochum for dysodia of the mouth (Part 1, Section 5, ref Eup. 1.80.1). **Confirmed.**
- plektikon: Malabathron as plektikon te osme (Part 1, Section 2, ref 1.12.2); peucedanum root as plere osmes (Part 4, Section 10, ref 3.78.4). **Confirmed.**
- baryosmon-bromodes: Aloe as baryosmos (Part 1, Section 3, ref 3.22.1); sori as bromodes (Part 1, Section 4, ref 5.102.1). **Confirmed.**
- izo-verbs-adulteration: Nardizonta for phu root (Part 2, Section 6, ref 1.11.1); kastorizon for onyx shells (Part 2, Section 6, ref 2.8.1); peganizousa for cinnamon (Part 2, Section 6, ref 1.14.1); apoballo ten dysodian for fat purification (Part 4, Section 11, ref 2.76.6). **Confirmed.**
- filling-the-nose: Peripneonta kai plerounta ten osphresin for cinnamon (Part 3, Section 8, ref 1.14.2); pneon for iris perfume (Part 3, Section 9, ref 1.56.3); sagapenum osphranomenon (Part 3, Section 7, ref 3.81.2). **Confirmed.**

One note: the draft for izo-verbs-adulteration says "Beef fat for perfumery must be boiled and sun-bleached until it sheds its animal smell completely." The source (Part 4, Section 11) describes the fat being brought into the sun covered so it whitens and casts off its stink. The draft says "sun-bleached" which loosely captures this but slightly overstates the mechanism -- the source says "brought [into the sun] covered." Minor paraphrase, not a fidelity violation, but worth noting for precision.

## Batch-Level Notes

**Cross-post variety across the batch**: Four of five posts follow the same arc -- introduce a Greek term, explain its etymology/usage, closer lands a reframe. This is the dominant shape in the DEAD WORDS series already. The batch would benefit from at least one post that breaks this pattern -- e.g., a scene-based post (a buyer at a spice stall using the plektikon test), a process post (the sequence of grading from weak to strong), or a mystery-arc post. As written, the batch is internally varied enough (binary, physical, taxonomic, linguistic, survey) but the overall arc shape repeats.

**Strongest entries**: euodia-dysodes and baryosmon-bromodes are ready to publish. Both have tight spines, earned body slides, and closers that reframe without restating.

**Priority fixes**: filling-the-nose needs the most work (structural rebuild). plektikon and izo-verbs-adulteration need targeted cuts and closer rewrites.
