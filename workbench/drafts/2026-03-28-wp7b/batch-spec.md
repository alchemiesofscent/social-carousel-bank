## What to emphasize

- Repetitive craft actions: grinding, stirring, wiping, covering, sealing.
- Controlled exposure versus airtight closure.
- Trade vocabulary as evidence for real shop-space.
- Cautious archaeology: signs, stock, traces, and vocabulary instead of one standard plan.

## What to avoid

- A generic “ancient laboratory” explainer.
- Treating every cloth as a filter or every seal as permanent storage.
- Claiming the Brun/Fernandez PDF gives a complete floorplan.
- Duplicating the existing `perfumers-shade` or `perfume-shops` posts.
