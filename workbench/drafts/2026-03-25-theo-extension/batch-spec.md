## Drafting notes

### `perfume-parts`
- Use named perfumes, not abstract taxonomy.
- Distinguish this from `roots-vs-flowers`: classification, not release mechanics.

### `perfume-colors`
- Use Theophrastus for colored vs uncolored logic.
- Use Pliny for technical color language and expected named colors.
- Keep the post about perfume finish and recognition, not about clothing or cosmetics.

### `oil-fit-perfume`
- Do not make this a generic “best oil” post.
- Lead with the idea of fit: some perfumes or perfume classes use one base, others another.
- Use `theophrastus-on-odors.txt:21` explicitly: rhodinon is best received by sesame because of its fattiness, even though heated sesame can smell of itself.
- Use Pliny and Dioscorides briefly as recipe witnesses for named pairings.
- Keep Dioscorides compact: mention his amarakinon and nardinum pairings only if they sharpen the fit argument.
- Keep `balanos-oil` distinct by avoiding the “chosen for absence” argument as the main hook.

### `rhodinon-sales-trick`
- Treat this as shop practice and sensory manipulation.
- Keep it separate from wrist-testing and garland medicine.
- Make the mechanism legible: rose fills the sense first, then fades.
