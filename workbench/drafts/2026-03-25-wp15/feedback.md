### balsam-grades

- Source fidelity: HARD FAIL on one claim. The hook states: "In Alexander's time, a full summer's harvest from a twenty-acre garden filled about six gallons." - Series fit: PASS — MATERIA is correct.
- Duplication: FLAG — balsam-judea already covers the twenty-acre garden, the collection method (wool tufts into small horns), the four-grade hierarchy mention, and the price context. Body 1 and Body 2 of this post significantly overlap with balsam-judea's body slides. The balsam-judea post frames balsam as a political story; this post is framed around grades and scarcity economics — but the supporting material (collection method, hierarchy quote) is nearly identical to what balsam-judea already shows. The writer should differentiate more sharply: either go deeper on the grade distinctions and their uses (xylobalsamum in compound unguentis, the seed vs. bark market) or drop the collection-method body slide (which balsam-judea covers) and replace it with something balsam-judea doesn't touch — such as the fraud/adulteration tests in NH 12.119–123.
