**`myrakopa`:**
- The word: μυράκοπα = compound perfumes with consistency of akopon (thick medicated ointment for muscles)
- Three-part taxonomy: simple oils → compound true oils (mura/aleimmata) → myrákopa (wax/resin-accepting)
- Named examples: nardinon, gleukinon, kyprinon = true oils; dekamyron, amarakinon = myrákopa
- The boundary between luxury and medicine ran through texture

**`perfume-fire`:**
- badge: "✦ WORKSHOP"
- Three-stage embole: hard aromatics (aspalathus, spathe, rosemary, laurel) → spices (phullon, nardostachys, amomum, brathys) → gums/fats/marrows
- Balsam sap exception: added to cooled perfume only, never boiled
- Mendesian exception: never boiled at all, only mixed
- The logic: heat kills volatile aromatics; the hardest perfume to make was the one that avoided fire
