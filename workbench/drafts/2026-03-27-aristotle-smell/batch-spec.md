## Drafting notes

### `smell-middle-sense`
- Keep the post conceptual rather than medical.
- Lead with the surprise that Aristotle will not let smell stay in one sensory category.
- Use air-and-water plus middle-sense language as the main evidence.
- Do not drift back into flowers, pleasant smells, brain cooling, or human health.

### `animal-smell-distance`
- Keep the post zoological rather than abstract.
- Use concrete animals early: fish, insects, bees, ants, sea creatures.
- The point is not "animals smell better"; the point is that Aristotle already treats smell as species-specific.
- Use the eyelid analogy only to clarify difference in apparatus, not to claim smell is equivalent to vision.
