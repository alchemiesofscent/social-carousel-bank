```text
Source: Cleidemus, Exegetikon (quoted passage); subject: Athenian funeral and purification rites

It is specifically called aponymma among the Athenians in reference to the rites performed in honor of the dead and to the purifications of those who have been polluted by death, as Cleidemus also notes in his work titled Exegetikon. For when introducing the section on funeral offerings, he writes the following:

“Dig a pit on the west side of the grave; then, facing west beside the pit, pour out water, saying these words: ‘This is the aponymma for you, to whom it is due and to whom it is lawful.’ Then again pour out perfume.”
```
